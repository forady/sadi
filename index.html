for the days when everything feels a little heavy

For you, Baby.

A little place you can come back to whenever you need me.

Hi, baby.

If you’re reading this, maybe you’re having one of those days again. Maybe you’re sad, your mind is getting too loud, or a part of you suddenly feels like pushing everything away.

So before you do anything, just stay here with me for a bit.

You know, sometimes I think about everything you’ve told me, and I wonder how much more there is that you never had the words to explain. I know you’ve been hurt in ways I probably can’t even imagine. And maybe some things hurt for so long that eventually, you just got used to them. You learned to live around the pain until one day you started telling yourself, “Okay ra ko.”

Then I saw you cry.

And baby… sakit kaayo to tan-awon.

Lamia sumbagon sa ni dapat nimo oi. 😭

Because while I was looking at you, all I could think was, how could someone make this woman feel this way?

No woman deserves to be treated like that. You didn’t deserve to be treated like that.

I don’t know why you stayed for as long as you did. Maybe you loved him. Maybe you kept hoping things would change. Maybe walking away from someone who had been part of your life for years was harder than anyone else could understand.

Whatever the reason, I will never judge you for it.

I’m just happy that eventually, you left.

Because somewhere after that chapter of your life ended, somehow…

we met.

And ambot nimo, pero dili gyud ko mutuo nga coincidence ra na. HAHAHAHA.

There were millions of people we could’ve met. So many little things could’ve happened differently and our lives could have completely missed each other.

But they didn’t.

I found you.

And baby, look at our picture for a second.

Look at us. 🥹

I don’t know why, but this picture makes me so happy. It’s nothing grand. It’s literally just us, caught in one small moment together but maybe that’s exactly why I love it.

Because look at us, baby. Look at how comfortable we are with each other. Look at that little moment we got to have.

Sometimes I look at pictures like this and think, “Hays ka cute dyud namo oiiii”

And maybe your mind will sometimes try to convince you that something this good is something you should be afraid of. Maybe it’ll tell you to pull away before you can get hurt again.

When that happens, look at this picture.

Not because a picture can promise what happens tomorrow.

But because it can remind you of what was true in that moment:

You were loved. You were safe with me. And I was so damn happy to be beside you.

That moment was real, baby.

And you deserve to have moments like this without constantly wondering when they’re going to disappear.

I wish I could answer every fear you have. I wish I could take everything that happened, make sense of it, and somehow remove all the little things it left behind.

Pero I can’t, baby.

And maybe I’m not supposed to.

I wasn’t brought into your life to erase everything that happened before me.

Maybe all I can do is be here for everything that comes after.

So I’m not going to ask you to suddenly stop overthinking. I’m not going to expect you to wake up one day completely okay.

If you’re scared, tell me.

If you’re sad, come to me.

And if you don’t even know what’s wrong and you just want someone beside you, naa ra ko.

You don’t always have to know what to say. You don’t have to pretend you’re okay around me.

And if one day your mind tells you to run from me again, come back here first.

Look at our picture.

Look at yourself in that little moment with me.

And remember:

That girl deserves to know what it feels like to be happy without being afraid of what comes after.

You deserve that, baby.

I don’t know where life will take us, and I don’t have some magical answer for everything you’re going through.

But I know what I can give you.

My patience.
My understanding.
My hand to hold.
And someone who genuinely wants to stay beside you while you figure things out.

You’ve spent so much time carrying things on your own.

You don’t have to do that with me.

Naa na ko diri, baby.

So when everything gets a little too heavy again, come back here.

Look at our picture.

Read this again.

And imagine me beside you telling you the same thing I’d tell you if I were there:

“Come here, baby. Naa ra ko. We’ll figure this out together.”

I love you. 🤍
